# Creator Playbook: Corporate Girl Who Golfs

A personal creator account about a millennial in hotel sales teaching herself golf. It builds an audience, earns from brand work and affiliate links, and becomes the launch channel for Fairway Society in February 2027.

**At a glance**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Account type | Personal creator account (not the brand account) |
| Audience | Global, mostly women, open to everyone |
| Style | Faceless for now: voiceover, hands, outfits, POV shots. Face reveal later, planned as a milestone. |
| Platforms | TikTok (growth), Instagram (brand deals), Facebook Reels (reach and payouts) |
| Cadence | 1 reel a day, filmed in two batch sessions a week |
| Budget | About S$300 to start |
| Income goal | S$3,000 a month |

## 1. Positioning

**One line:** *Hotel sales by day, golf by 6pm. Self-taught, shooting 120, and learning that the course is the best meeting room there is.*

What makes this account different from other golf accounts:

1. **She's a beginner, not a pro.** Most golf content is made by good golfers. Scoring 120 and getting better in public is easier for viewers to relate to.
2. **Golf is a work skill for her.** In hotel sales, client entertainment is part of the job, so the golf has a real reason behind it.
3. **She works in hospitality.** She can talk about golf travel, golf hotels and business trips from the inside. Global viewers care about this, and travel brands pay well for it.

## 3. Filming without showing your face

**Shots that work without a face:**
- Outfit changes filmed from the neck down (blazer comes off, golf glove goes on)
- Close-ups of your hands: gloves, tees, a coffee next to your laptop, a scorecard
- Tripod behind you at the range: the swing, the ball flight, the course ahead
- POV from chest height: walking into the office, then walking onto the first tee
- Items laid flat: work tote next to golf bag, packing a suitcase
- On-screen text with a voiceover for storytimes and tips

**Voiceover:** record it in a closet or under a blanket with the clip mic. Speak as if you're sending a voice note to a friend, not reading a script.

**Face reveal:** plan it as an event when you reach 10k followers on any platform ("You've been watching my hands for 4 months..."). Reveals usually get a lot of engagement.

## 4. Hook bank: 30 reel ideas

Each idea lists the hook (said in the first 1–2 seconds and shown on screen), the shots, and the call to action at the end. Keep reels to 7–25 seconds.

**Posting across platforms:** post on TikTok first, then Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels the same day. Download without the TikTok watermark (export from CapCut) because Instagram shows fewer watermarked videos. Post at your audience's peak time. For a global audience, try 7–9pm Singapore time (morning in the US) and 12–1pm Singapore time (evening in Europe), then check your analytics after two weeks.

**Every day (10 minutes):** reply to every comment in the first hour, and post one Story (range clip, poll or behind-the-scenes).

## 6. Plan for S$3,000 a month

An honest note first: an account with few followers won't make S$3,000 a month from platform payouts or sponsored posts in month 1. The fastest way to earn early is **UGC**, where brands pay you to make videos for *their* accounts and ads, based on your filming skills rather than your follower count. Faceless, hands-and-outfits content is exactly what UGC brands buy.

| Income stream | Starts | Realistic range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **UGC for brands** (golf, activewear, workwear, travel, skincare) | Month 1 | S$150–400 per video; 5–8 videos/month = **S$1,000–3,000** | Make 3 sample videos first, put them in a free portfolio (Canva or Notion page), and pitch 20 brands a week. You can also find briefs on UGC marketplaces (e.g. Billo, Insense, JoinBrands). |
| **Affiliate links** (outfits, golf gear, travel) | Month 1 | S$50–300/month at first, growing with views | Use LTK or ShopMy for outfits, and Amazon Associates plus golf brands' own programmes for gear. Put the links on your bio link page and mention them in reels: "link in bio". |
| **Paid posts on your own account** | About 5k followers (month 2–4) | S$200–800 per reel at 5–15k followers | Golf ranges, simulator venues, activewear, golf-travel and hotel brands. |
| **Hotel and resort collaborations** | About 10k followers | Free stays at first, paid later | Check for conflicts with your employer first (see Rules). |
| **Platform payouts** | Once you qualify | Small at first | Check whether you're eligible *as a Singapore resident* for the TikTok Creator Rewards Program, Facebook's content monetisation and YouTube Shorts revenue sharing. The rules differ by country and change often. Treat payouts as a bonus, not the plan. |
| **Fairway Society sales** | February 2027 | The biggest long-term earner | Roughly 150 polos a month at S$99 already gives about S$3,000+ profit a month at your planned markup. |

**Targets:**
- Month 1: 30 reels posted, UGC portfolio live, 60 brand pitches sent, first S$300–1,000.
- Month 3: 5k followers on your best platform, UGC work coming in regularly, first paid post. **S$1,500–3,000.**
- Month 6: 15–25k followers, affiliate income, 2–4 paid posts a month. **S$3,000+ across all streams.**
- February 2027: Fairway Society launches to an audience you've already built.

## 7. Measuring progress

Check every Sunday (15 minutes):

| Metric | Aim for | If it's low |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3-second hold rate | 60%+ | Rewrite the hook: start mid-action and put the text on screen straight away |
| Average watch time | 50%+ of the reel's length | Make the reel shorter and cut the pauses |
| Shares + saves per 1,000 views | 10+ | Make more "send this to…" and "save for…" reels (pillars B and E) |
| New followers per reel | Rising week to week | Add series and "part 2" endings |

Make more of the top 2 reels each week (same format, new topic) and stop the bottom 2 formats.

## 8. Rules

- **Your employer:** read your company's social media policy. Never name your employer, clients or deal details, and anonymise every storytime. Hotel collaborations with competitors of your employer could be a conflict of interest, so check before you accept one.
- **Disclosing paid posts:** turn on the platform's "paid partnership" or branded-content label and add `#ad` on every paid post. Follow Singapore's ASAS guidelines on influencer marketing, and the rules of your audience's countries.
- **Music:** Instagram and Facebook business accounts only get the commercial music library. Use a *personal creator* account and trending audio from inside each app, not uploaded songs.
- **Brand accounts:** keep the personal account and `@fairwaysociety` separate, and tag the brand account in pillar F content.
