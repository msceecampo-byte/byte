# Week 1 Scripts

Seven reels ready to film, one for each day of Week 1 in the calendar (`PLAYBOOK.md`, section 5), plus three sample videos for your UGC portfolio.

**Before you film:**
- Film everything vertically (9:16), in 4K or 1080p at 30fps.
- Record voiceovers separately in a quiet room with the clip mic, then add them in CapCut.
- Put the first line of on-screen text in the **top third** of the frame and turn on auto-captions. Most people watch without sound.
- Replace `@yourhandle` with your chosen handle.
- Never show your employer's name, logo, uniform, building or colleagues.

**Filming plan:**
| Session | Where | Reels it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session 1, weekday evening (60–90 min) | Office bathroom or car, then the driving range | #4, #18, plus B-roll for #7 and #14 |
| Session 2, weekend (2 hours) | Home: wardrobe, bed or floor for flat lays, plus 30 min at the range | #5, #20, #27, plus UGC samples |

**B-roll to collect at every range session** (short clips to use across many reels): glove going on, tee going into the ground, a slow-motion swing from behind, the ball flying against the sky, a bucket of balls, a scorecard and pencil, walking with the bag, a sunset over the range.

## UGC portfolio samples (film these in Session 2)

Make three sample videos with things you already own. Don't show brand logos and don't tag the brands. They're for your portfolio, not to pretend you've worked with the brand.

### Sample 1 · Golf accessory "in my routine" (golf glove, tees, ball marker or bag)
*About 20 seconds, voiceover.*
- **Hook (0–2s):** "The one thing in my golf bag I never forget."
- **Shots:** close-ups of hands using the product; in the bag; on the course.
- **Voiceover:** what problem it solves, one detail you like (material, grip, colour), when you use it.
- **End:** "If you're just starting golf, get this first."

### Sample 2 · Sunscreen or skincare "office to outdoors"
*About 20 seconds. Sun protection matters on a golf course, and the brands spend a lot on ads.*
- **Hook (0–2s):** "Going from air-con all day to four hours in the sun? This is what I do."
- **Shots:** pump or squeeze in close-up, applying it to your hands and arms, a sun shot at the range.
- **Voiceover:** texture, whether it's sticky or greasy under a cap, reapplying at the turn (hole 9).
- **End:** "Your skin will thank you by hole 18."

### Sample 3 · Work bag or tote "fits my work stuff and my golf stuff"
*About 15 seconds, no voiceover, just text and audio.*
- **Hook (0–2s):** text **"Everything I need from the office to the range in one bag."**
- **Shots:** packing flat-lay: laptop, notebook, then glove, balls, a change of top. Zip it up, walk out.
- **End:** text **"Would you carry this?"**

Upload the three videos (unlisted YouTube or a Google Drive folder set to "anyone with the link") and add the links to your portfolio page.
