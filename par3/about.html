About PAR3

Not new to the game

PAR3 has been dressing golfers in Singapore for a long time. Now we're bringing the same kit to golfers everywhere.

PAR3 started in Singapore, where golf means heat, humidity and the odd tropical downpour. That shaped everything we make: fabrics that dry fast, stay cool and still look sharp in the clubhouse afterwards.

Our golfers aren't tour pros. They play on weekends, book early tee times before work and care about looking good without paying for a logo. That's who we design for: well-made golf apparel at a fair price.

This new store makes it easier to shop PAR3 from anywhere, with prices in your currency, clear delivery times and a size finder that takes the guesswork out of ordering online.
