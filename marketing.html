Words to reuse

Before posting

  • Swap the garment illustrations for campaign photos once they're shot. The layouts in marketing/kit.html take a photo in the same slot.
  • Add the real Instagram handle and waitlist link wherever a caption says “link in bio”.
  • Put the poll sticker on the “This or that?” story inside Instagram.
  • Print the poster at A5, the thank-you card at 5 × 7 in and the hang tag at 2 × 4 in.

Keep it on brand

  • Warm, quietly confident, never exclusive. The society is earned by playing, not by who you know.
  • Use the script for short accents only: a date, a sign-off, a single phrase.
  • Spell the Singapore way: colour, odour, favourite.
  • Prices in S$. Only claim what the fabric does: UPF 50+, moisture-wicking, 4-way stretch.
